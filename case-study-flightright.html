The Problem

Revenue was capped by a narrow market — only 3 in 100 flights qualify for EU261 compensation. Growing case volume alone wasn't enough. We needed to lift unit economics on the cases we already had, without scaring off price-sensitive claimants.

"If we show fees too early, we lose the customer. If we hide them, we leave money on the table." — Growth review, Q2
engineering

The Process

  • check_circleHypothesis: showing lawyer fees post-POA signing increases trust and avoids deducting from payouts
  • check_circleVariant B placed fee transparency after the Power of Attorney signature, the highest-commitment step
  • check_circleConversion guardrail set at −5% relative — abort if Variant B underperforms
  • check_circleTracked at Day 1, 3, 7, and 14 to capture early signals and longer-tail behavior
  • check_circlePre-registered stop conditions and analysis plan with the data team

The Proof

+27% GMV

Variant B lifted GMV by 27% — pure expansion on existing cases, no extra acquisition cost.

Conversion held flat

No statistically significant difference across both groups at Day 1, 3, 7, or 14. Guardrail respected.

Funnel preserved

Post-POA timing meant fee transparency only landed once trust was already established.

3 in 100 flights

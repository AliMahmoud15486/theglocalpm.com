Common questions

What skills separate a Senior PM from a junior one? expand_more

Strategic synthesis (connecting market signals to vision), stakeholder leverage (turning conflict into alignment), technical literacy (engineering trade-offs), and analytical rigor (SQL, funnels, hypothesis testing). Senior PMs operate ambiguously and ship outcomes, not output.

How does AI change the PM skill stack? expand_more

AI automates the "glue" work — PRDs, ticket triage, status syncs — and rewards higher-order skills: clear problem definition, ethical guardrails, prompt engineering, and orchestrating AI agents alongside human teammates. See the Agent Lab for concrete examples.

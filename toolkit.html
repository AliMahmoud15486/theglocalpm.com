Tooling questions, answered

What tools should a Senior Product Manager use in 2025? expand_more

A modern Senior PM stack covers six areas: design (Figma), project management (Jira or Linear), analytics (Mixpanel or Amplitude), knowledge (Notion), communication (Slack), and engineering (GitHub for PR reviews and release tracking). Layer AI tools on top — ChatGPT, Claude, and custom GPTs — for PRD drafting and competitive research.

Why should a PM use GitHub? expand_more

Reviewing PRs and tracking releases keeps PMs close to engineering reality. You spot tech-debt trade-offs early, ship release notes faster, and earn credibility with the team by speaking the same language.

Is Notion or Confluence better for PRDs? expand_more

Notion wins on speed, flexibility, and embedded databases for backlog → PRD linking. Confluence is better when you're in a heavily Atlassian-integrated org. The right answer is the one your engineering team will actually read.
